2. **Drain / Migration**:
   If an arena authority needs to decommission an inactive battle room, call `reclaim_invariants` after all stakes are resolved.

3. **RPC Failover**:
   The agent daemon accepts comma-separated fallback RPC endpoints. In `.env`:
   `SOLANA_RPC_URL=https://mainnet.helius-rpc.com/?api-key=xxx,https://rpc.ankr.com/solana`
